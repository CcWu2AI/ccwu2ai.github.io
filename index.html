Our operating philosophy 我们的运营理念

Enterprise AI should strengthen judgment—not replace responsibility. 企业 AI 应增强判断，而不是取代责任。

We build systems around the reality of organizations: fragmented knowledge, high-stakes decisions, strict permissions and the need for measurable outcomes. 我们围绕组织真实需求构建系统：分散的知识、高风险决策、严格权限以及可衡量的业务结果。

01 / GOVERN

Control before scale.先治理，再规模化。

Permissions, review paths, auditability and clear ownership are designed before expansion.在扩展之前设计权限、审核路径、可审计性与明确责任。

02 / INTEGRATE

Work with the enterprise.融入企业，而非脱离企业。

AI connects to existing workflows, knowledge and decision structures instead of creating another isolated tool.AI 与现有工作流、知识和决策结构连接，而不是制造新的信息孤岛。

03 / PROVE

Measure what matters.衡量真正重要的价值。

Systems are evaluated on realistic tasks, operational quality and business outcomes—not demonstrations alone.系统依据真实任务、运营质量与业务结果进行评估，而不仅是演示效果。

From possibility to operation 从可能性走向运营

A disciplined path from AI ambition to enterprise value. 以严谨路径将 AI 愿景转化为企业价值。

01

Define the decision.定义决策。

Start with the workflow, responsibility and outcome—not the model.从工作流、责任与结果出发，而不是从模型出发。

02

Connect trusted context.连接可信语境。

Bring together approved knowledge, tools and enterprise permissions.整合经过批准的知识、工具与企业权限。

03

Evaluate realistic work.评估真实工作。

Test quality, safety and usefulness against operating conditions.根据真实运营条件测试质量、安全与实用性。

04

Scale with accountability.在可问责基础上规模化。

Expand only when controls, ownership and monitoring are ready.只有当控制、责任归属和监控准备就绪时才扩展。
